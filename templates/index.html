Solution Playbook

Strategies to Mitigate Air Pollution

Convert dashboard intelligence into action. The following focus areas combine policy, technology, and community engagement to accelerate cleaner air outcomes.

Prioritize interventions where pollutant averages and hotspots are highest.
Policy & Governance

Strengthen regional emission standards

  • Deploy state-wide clean air action plans tied to dashboard metrics.
  • Enforce seasonal bans on biomass burning and diesel restrictions in urban cores.
  • Offer fiscal incentives for industries that meet or beat tightened AQI targets.
Technology & Infrastructure

Invest in smarter, cleaner systems

  • Scale electric public transport and last-mile e-mobility networks.
  • Adopt IoT-based continuous monitoring to feed real-time alerts.
  • Upgrade power plants and brick kilns with low-emission retrofits.
Community & Behaviour

Empower citizens to contribute

  • Launch hyperlocal air-quality advisories via schools and RWAs.
  • Run behavioural campaigns on waste segregation and no-burn zones.
  • Support urban greening and rooftop gardens in high AQI wards.
Research & Funding

Sustain long-term innovation

  • Fund source apportionment studies in emerging hotspots.
  • Partner with climate-tech start-ups on low-cost sensor grids.
  • Channel CSR budgets toward clean cooking, waste-to-energy, and air sheds.
